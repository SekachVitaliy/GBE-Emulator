# Records: what is included and what is not

## Included
- `conversations/`: cleaned, readable export (hook/system noise removed, long tool results truncated, agent prompts kept in full).
- `raw-transcripts/`: redacted raw transcripts of the lead session, all nine subagents, and the early exploratory sessions (details and redactions in its README).
- `snapshots/`: twelve source snapshots (one per new best score).
- `../.claude-flow/data/`: the autopilot checklist and the score history (`scores.md`, one line per graded run).
- `../gba-emu/results/final/`: per-testcase results of the final graded run.
- `USAGE.md`, `WORKFLOW.md`: time, tokens, cost (partial) and how the work was organised.

## Not available, and why
- **Version control history.** None exists. The only intermediate code states are the twelve snapshots (sparse).
- **Most of the model's reasoning.** Claude Code stored reasoning blocks only partially: in the lead session 51 of 230 blocks are non-empty, in the nine subagent sessions all are empty. The final messages, tool calls and results are complete.
- **Grader outputs of earlier runs.** About 9.8 GB of audio (WAV) and image outputs from 41 intermediate grading runs were deleted to free disk space (with the owner's approval). Kept: the final run's per-testcase JSON, `scores.md`, the checklist.
- **Records after the cut point.** The lead transcript ends before the messages that prepared the export and the customer correspondence; two helper sessions containing that correspondence are omitted.
- **Unrelated chats.** A separate export of the owner's claude.ai web chats exists; it contains no development history and unrelated personal content, so it is not included.
- **Redacted values.** Account, organisation and bridge-session identifiers, e-mail addresses, the OS user name, and the account's list of unrelated sessions. Redaction is mechanical and keeps the structure of each record.
- **Local tool state.** The ruflo memory database and caches (local state, not a record of the conversation). The 89-agent default library generated by `ruflo init` is not included; the agents actually used and their prompts are in the transcripts.
- **Human time.** About 2-3 hours, self-reported; not measured.
- **Mesen2 / other sources.** Questions about which sources were consulted are answered separately.
